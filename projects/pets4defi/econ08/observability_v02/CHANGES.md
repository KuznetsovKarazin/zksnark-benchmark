# Changes from pilot v0.1

* Added explicit cache-architecture assumptions and proofs of common-invalidation
  necessity, minimum full-authorization count, fixed-coefficient gas optimality,
  and the conditional availability boundary. Added a varying-price counterexample.
* Kept the two-sided support guard as a supporting lemma with prior-art context.
* Added one independent coin per foreign publication; a failed coin is not retried.
  Exact sequential inference is checked against enumeration of hidden coin states.
* Corrected the proposed epsilon interpretation: the hit outcome has a zero
  likelihood under an own mandatory update. No finite pure-epsilon frontier.
* Added exact weekly expectations over 21 rho values and all 4096 response strings.
* Retained all v0.1 timing controls and expanded model grace to 3/7/14 days.
* Added anonymous-publication cohort clustering, shuffled public handles, separate
  ground truth, missing-opportunity handling, one external anchor and false-attribution
  metrics. No claim that the modeled channel exists in EF's off-chain design.
* Retained the complete outage grid and made its stress assumptions visible in a figure.
* Added three economic cases, 96 coalition quantities and two compensation references.
  All transfers balance before rounding; event differences retain negative credits.
* Added a small English task for Nataliya. Her completed liquidity result is preserved;
  she is not asked to find a positive pooling result, review cryptography or build EVM code.
* Corrected privacy-premium denominators: +32.33% versus selective, or a 24.43%
  saving when moving in the opposite direction. Costs remain modeled access-gas proxies.

The old claim that common epochs dominate all randomized relaxations is not used.
The strong guarantees and empirical attack findings are reported under their exact
conditions; scientific novelty is not certified by a passing test suite.
