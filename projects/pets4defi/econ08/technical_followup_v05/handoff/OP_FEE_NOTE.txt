ECON-08 v0.5 — conditional Optimism fee scenario
15 November 2026. For information and economic interpretation.

WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN
We priced the existing B2 weekly and no-update transactions using three
components: execution, publication of transaction data to L1, and the OP
operator fee. The operator fee returned zero at this specific historical
state; it was queried, not assumed absent.

For four holders and 400 accesses over 90 days:
                                      Weekly updates       No updates
Holder-operation expense (EUR)           0.008223311        0.007296680
Shared deployment/publications (EUR)     0.004409694        0.004162828
Whole measured service (EUR)             0.012633005        0.011459508
Daily holder pool target, full life      0.000433877        0.000433878
Daily pool target, service after day 1   0.000247057        0.000298652
Sum of SELF targets, after day 1         0.000331193        0.000298652

The weekly case has greater cumulative expense but a smaller continuing
service pool target. Its pooling reduction is about EUR 0.000084136 for all
four holders. These very small absolute amounts do not support a claim that
the tested L2 direct-gas scenario alone creates an important funding problem.
An operating balance is an asset; neither its deposit nor its replenishment
is an additional resource expense. Startup remains separately payable in
the after-day-1 comparison.

HOW THE PROJECTION WAS MADE
The read-only calculation uses OP mainnet block 158458797, timestamp
20 October 2026, 17:12:51 UTC, hash:
0x4751aa2942644e95811e66e09100ca1cc5218b563aa11ae6620dba1bf4bd0764.
The original archived fee snapshot records the same block. Its block-weighted
median of within-block execution-price p50 values is 6,661 wei per gas.
That historical window statistic, not a live quote, prices every event here.
EUR/ETH remains the original dated input, 2,164.73.

Execution = original local-EVM gas used multiplied by 6,661 wei.
Data fee = historical GasPriceOracle.getL1Fee(unsigned transaction).
Operator fee = historical GasPriceOracle.getOperatorFee(gas used proxy).
The oracle address is 0x420000000000000000000000000000000000000F.
Type-2 transaction envelopes retain original destination, calldata, value,
nonce and gas limit; the chain ID is 10 and fee fields are the stated OP
scenario. Every fee is calculated before conversion to EUR. Archived RPC
requests/responses allow offline reconstruction of all 848 quotes.

BOUNDARIES
This is not execution on OP and not measurement of an ERC-4337 paymaster.
Original gas usage is a proxy; the same pinned oracle state prices all
90-day events. Future volatility, OP-specific execution changes, bundling,
validation, sponsor policy, failed operations and refill overhead/delay are
not measured. The cash targets are hindsight debit floors and cannot be
advertised as safe production deposits. These two B2 cases do not establish
that funding constraints can never matter on L2 or at larger scale.
